# CrateBot Design Partner Outreach Brief

## The Context

You're reaching out to DJs you already know. These aren't cold messages — they're personal asks to friends and acquaintances who happen to have the exact problem CrateBot solves.

**Goal:** 5-10 design partners in 2-3 weeks
**Ask:** Use CrateBot on your real library, tell me what breaks
**NOT asking (yet):** Testimonials, public endorsements, promotion

---

## The Core Message

The message needs to hit three notes:

1. **Personal connection** — why you're reaching out to *them*
2. **The problem they recognize** — tagging is tedious, everyone knows this
3. **Low-commitment ask** — try it, give feedback, that's it

---

## Outreach Templates

### Template 1: The Direct Ask (For Close Contacts)

**Subject:** Quick favor — need a DJ to break my new tool

---

Hey [Name],

I built something I think you'll actually find useful — an app that learns how you tag your music and then does it automatically for new tracks.

You know the drill: you download 50 new tracks, and before you can use them in a set you've got to sit there tagging genre, energy, mood, whatever your system is. It's tedious as hell and everyone hates it.

CrateBot watches how you've tagged your existing library, learns your patterns, and applies them to new files. Train it once on your collection, then just point it at new downloads.

I'm looking for a few DJs to try it on their real libraries before I release it more widely. Not looking for you to promote it or anything — I just need people to break it and tell me what sucks.

Would you be down to try it on your setup and give me honest feedback? It's a desktop app, works on Mac, takes maybe 10 minutes to train on your library.

[Link to landing page]

Let me know — and if it's not your thing, no worries at all.

[Your name]

## Onboarding: What to Say When They Agree

Once someone says yes:

---

Awesome — thanks for being willing to try it.

Here's the download: [link]

Setup takes about 5 minutes, and training on your library depends on size (a few hundred tracks = ~10 min).

What I'm specifically looking for:

- **What breaks?** Any crashes, weird behavior, errors
- **What's confusing?** Anything in the UI that doesn't make sense
- **What's wrong?** Tags that are clearly incorrect or that you'd never apply
- **What's missing?** Features you expected that aren't there

Don't worry about being nice — the whole point is to find problems before more people use it.

I'll check in with you in a week or so to see how it's going. And if you hit a wall or give up, just tell me why — that's useful info too.

Thanks again.

[Your name]

---

## Feedback Collection

### Check-In Questions (After ~1 Week)

1. Did you actually use it? (No judgment — if not, why not?)
2. What did it get right?
3. What did it get obviously wrong?
4. Did it crash or behave weirdly anywhere?
5. Anything confusing about the setup or UI?
6. Would you keep using it as-is, or does it need work first?
7. What would make it twice as useful?

---

## What NOT to Ask (Yet)

Don't ask for:

- Public testimonials
- Social media posts
- Referrals to other DJs
- Reviews or ratings

Earn the right to ask for those by first delivering value. Fix their issues, make it work well for them, *then* ask if they'd be willing to say something publicly.
