01

Intelligent Tagging

Trained on your library, tuned to your taste. CrateBot learns how you categorize music and predicts tags for new tracks.

Custom ML Models

Train classifiers on your own tagged library. The model learns your vocabulary, your categories, your way of thinking about music.

Multi-Label Taxonomy

Four-field system: Genre, Timing, Mood, and Descriptive. Each track can have multiple tags per field for nuanced classification.

Vibe Generation

AI-crafted descriptions like "DARK FLUTE MELODY PEAK" and synesthetic mnemonics like "sweating serpent" for instant recall.

Hook Detection

Finds the memorable vocal phrase in each track. Uses lyrics databases first, falls back to speech-to-text transcription.

Batch Processing

Tag thousands of tracks while you sleep. Checkpoint recovery means you can stop and resume without losing progress.

Manual Refinement

Review predictions with inline audio playback. Corrections persist across model updates and sync to your files.

Three Steps to Smarter Crates

From raw audio to rich metadata in a streamlined workflow.

1

Train

Point CrateBot at your already-tagged library. It extracts 135+ audio features and learns your classification patterns.

2

Tag

Batch process new tracks. CrateBot predicts genre, mood, timing, and descriptive tags—plus generates creative vibes.

3

Refine

Review predictions with audio playback. Make corrections, train again. Your model improves with each iteration.
